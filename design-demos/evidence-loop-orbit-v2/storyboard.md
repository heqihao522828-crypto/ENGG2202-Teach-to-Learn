# Evidence loop orbit · lightweight motion card

This is a single continuous website interaction, not a video or a separate
scene sequence. The camera remains still. The orbit is the only motion.

| # | Time | Shot scale | Camera + reason | Composition | Key motion | Transition | Acceptance frame |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–32s loop | Full view 1x | Static, because the user needs to read five concepts while their relationship moves | Centre statement occupies 30%; circular track occupies 76%; five cards sit at equal 72-degree intervals; all content remains inside a 10% safe area | The track and five card-position wrappers rotate clockwise 360 degrees; each card rotates counter-clockwise 360 degrees so its text remains upright; hover pauses both motions | Seamless return to frame 0 | 0s / 8s / 16s / 24s / 31.9s |

Reduced-motion mode keeps the same five-card composition but removes all
rotation. The image is decorative support; the text carries the meaning and
must retain AA contrast.
