# Gate 01: Focus

## Decision

Which Green Technology challenge is worth investigating next?

Gate 01 chooses a direction, not a final solution. "Build a robot" or "use AI" describes a technology interest, not a challenge.

## What to do

1. Discuss the team's interests, experience and skills to develop.
2. Read relevant [UN SDG goals and targets](https://sdgs.un.org/goals).
3. Find at least three possible challenge directions.
4. Compare them using the same criteria.
5. Select one provisional direction for Gate 02.

For each direction, record:

- a reachable context;
- a possible stakeholder;
- an initial source or observation;
- the relevant SDG target;
- the main assumption or unanswered question;
- evidence the team could collect.

## Checkpoint submission

Submit one team post on Ed Discussion with:

1. selected challenge direction;
2. SDG goal, target and connection;
3. context and possible stakeholder;
4. comparison of at least three directions;
5. facts and assumptions kept separate;
6. questions for Gate 02;
7. direct links to repository evidence.

## Ready for Gate 02

- The team can explain the selected direction.
- The context and possible stakeholder are clear.
- The SDG connection uses a target, not only an icon.
- Alternatives were compared.
- The team knows what it must validate next.
