# Gate 02: Define

## Decision

Is this a real, important and bounded problem, and what evidence supports it?

Gate 02 tests the direction selected at Gate 01. Evidence may change the problem or stop the project from following its first idea.

## What to do

1. Choose one primary stakeholder and a reachable context.
2. Collect evidence through observation, conversation, measurement, public data or credible sources.
3. Establish a baseline for the current situation.
4. Examine existing products, services, research and open-source approaches.
5. Write a problem statement that does not assume a solution.
6. Draft measurable success criteria.

Ask permission before recording people. Do not collect personal data you do not need or upload private information to an external AI service.

## Problem statement

> [Stakeholder] needs to [activity or decision] in [context], but currently [evidence-backed difficulty]. This matters because [consequence]. The project will focus on [scope] and will not cover [exclusion].

## Resources

| Need | Resource | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Academic and technical sources | [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) and [HKU Libraries](https://lib.hku.hk/) | Find prior research and established methods |
| Local baseline data | [DATA.GOV.HK](https://data.gov.hk/en/) | Find Hong Kong datasets |
| Learn from selected documents | [DeepTutor](https://github.com/Active-Learning-Kyle/DeepTutor) | Ask questions across papers, reports or datasheets and trace answers back to the material |
| Research planning | [HKU GenAI](https://genai.hku.hk/) | Review search terms, interview questions and unsupported claims |
| Existing open projects | [GitHub search](https://github.com/search) | Find approaches, limitations, licences and reusable work |

DeepTutor and other AI tools can help the team read and organise material. They are not sources or stakeholders. Cite and verify the original documents.

## Checkpoint submission

Submit one team post on Ed Discussion with:

1. primary stakeholder and context;
2. evidence collection method;
3. baseline evidence;
4. existing approaches reviewed;
5. problem statement;
6. measurable success criteria;
7. remaining uncertainty;
8. direct links to repository evidence.

## Ready for Gate 03

- A reachable stakeholder and context are defined.
- The baseline uses evidence rather than assumption.
- Existing approaches have been reviewed.
- The problem statement does not prescribe a product.
- Success criteria can later be tested.
